# Cenote

## 1. Cover

**Cenote**

The privacy-first AI chief of staff for document-heavy work.

Local memory. Trusted retrieval. Action inside chat.

## 2. The Shift

AI is becoming part of day-to-day work, but the current stack breaks at the exact moment people need it to be trusted.

- Knowledge workers already rely on AI for drafting and summarization
- They still do not trust it with raw client, company, and personal context
- The result is fragmented workflows, shallow recall, and dropped follow-through
- The next winning AI product will not just be smarter
- It will be trusted enough to become part of the system of work

## 3. The Problem

Professionals already have the information they need. They cannot reliably use it when it matters.

- Context is scattered across notes, PDFs, messages, meeting summaries, and browser tabs
- Most AI tools are stateless and forgetful
- Workspace AI is trapped inside one SaaS
- Privacy-sensitive users do not want to upload their raw knowledge base into a generic cloud black box
- So AI gets used for one-off output, not durable operational leverage

## 4. The Opportunity

There is a large and growing class of professionals who want AI that remembers, retrieves, and follows through without crossing a hard privacy line.

- Recruiters
- Consultants
- Boutique agencies
- Founders
- Chiefs of staff
- Advisors
- Small professional-service teams

These users feel the pain immediately, buy faster than enterprise, and can expand into team workflows once the product becomes part of their daily habits.

## 5. The Product

Cenote turns private knowledge into durable operational memory.

- Indexes a local vault of notes and documents
- Processes sensitive files locally
- Builds long-term conversational memory
- Retrieves the right context when the user asks
- Surfaces tasks, entities, and follow-up
- Works through WhatsApp, Telegram, and web chat

This is not another chat wrapper.

It is a memory, retrieval, and action layer that sits between a user's knowledge base and daily communication flow.

## 6. Why It Wins

Cenote wins where trust, continuity, and workflow fit matter more than generic model access.

- Local-first ingestion keeps raw content on the user's machine
- The cloud layer only sees safe outputs, not the vault itself
- Every interaction becomes part of durable memory
- Messaging-native access fits how people already work
- Open-core architecture makes the privacy promise inspectable

The wedge is not "best generic AI."

The wedge is trusted memory with a real privacy boundary.

## 7. Why Now

The market is ready for an AI system that is both useful and architecturally trustworthy.

- AI adoption is now mainstream across business functions
- Agentic workflows are moving from experiment to production
- Privacy concerns around generative AI remain unresolved
- Existing products validate demand but leave a clear gap around local trust, durable memory, and message-native workflows

This is the window to define the trust-sensitive layer of applied AI before the category hardens around shallow cloud copilots.

The initial motion is narrow, founder-led, and high-touch.

- Weekly feedback loops
- Use product proof, case studies, and trust assets to expand

The first commercial goal is not mass signup volume.

It is retained, high-frequency usage inside a wedge that clearly pulls the product into daily workflow.

## 10. Business Model

Open-core distribution plus paid managed service.

- Community Edition: self-hosted local daemon, indexing, ingestion, simulator
- Pro: managed orchestration, messaging integration, onboarding, updates, support
- Team: shared memory, shared task extraction, admin controls
- Private deployment: premium support and controlled environments

This model fits the product.

Trust is earned through inspectability. Revenue comes from convenience, reliability, onboarding, and managed experience.

## 11. Moat

The moat is not local-first by itself.

The moat is the combination of:

- Privacy boundary built into the architecture
- Long-term memory tied to real work
- Message-native habit formation
- Workflow templates by vertical
- Accumulating context and follow-up history

The more Cenote becomes the system that remembers commitments and retrieves context at the right moment, the harder it becomes to rip out.
